## 4. Domain models

Added centralized `RegistrationStatus`, `AccountStatus`, `DocumentStatus`, `TeamAccount`, `TeamProfile`, `RegistrationDocument`, `VerificationHistory`, document requirements, registration transitions, lock rules, document approval checks, and registration summaries.

## 5. Repository and mutation architecture

All new writes use the existing `CompetitionRepository` and TanStack Query mutation hooks. The in-memory adapter supports demo team accounts, team profiles, team creation, player and official CRUD, private document metadata, submission validation, review actions, verification history, and audit records. Credential material is only available inside the explicitly demo/in-memory adapter and is never returned by normal account queries.

## 6. Team account architecture

Team accounts are separate from teams. Demo credentials authenticate through `authenticateTeam()`, active accounts redirect to `/team`, and disabled/suspended accounts are rejected. Production password hashing, sessions, RBAC enforcement, and ownership authorization remain deferred by scope.

## 7. Verification workflow

Players and officials use document metadata and review actions for approve, revision request, and reject. Revision reasons are preserved on document metadata; verification actions append history. Approved players become `ELIGIBLE` because that relationship already exists in the current Player model. Official approval remains separate from player eligibility.

## 8. Document architecture

Documents store type, filename, status, upload timestamp, private storage reference, reviewer metadata, and revision reason. No binary data or public asset URL is stored. The `storage_ref` is a demo abstraction ready for authenticated private storage later.

## 9. Permissions

The existing `permissions.ts` catalog was extended for team, account, player, official, document, and submission actions. No second permission catalog or raw role authorization path was introduced.
